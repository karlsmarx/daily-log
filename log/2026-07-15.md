# 2026-07-15

Added a new headless provisioning path to a device management flow, removing a browser-automation dependency from that flow entirely rather than just working around it.

Separately debugged a native binary crash in a legacy browser container: the binary depended on a dynamic linker that was bundled alongside it, but the launch wrapper was invoking the binary directly and letting the OS fall back to its own (incompatible) system linker instead. Fixed by making the launch wrapper invoke the bundled loader explicitly.
